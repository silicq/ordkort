# Ordkort roadmap

The best place to learn the words of a language: ready decks, a real dictionary, grammar and privacy,
closing the gaps to Anki one by one without losing lightness.

**What stays**: free, no account, works offline, end-to-end encrypted sync, open source. Languages first.
AI helps, real dictionaries check it. Everything has to work well on a phone.

## Now

| | What | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Remembering better.** Four answers (Again / Hard / Good / Easy) as an option next to the simple two; your own target retention; a history of every answer kept on the device; words you keep forgetting ("leeches") set aside instead of coming back forever. | done |
| 2 | **What goes wrong.** A page that reads your answers: words you confuse with each other, articles and forms you get wrong, the modes and directions that are hard for you, too many new words for your retention. Each finding comes with one thing to do about it. | done |
| 3 | **Pictures on cards.** Your own photo, a pasted image, or a free picture found by the word (Openverse). Kept on the device. | done |
| 4 | **Anki both ways.** Import an `.apkg` (notes, scheduling, pictures and sound), export your decks as an `.apkg` Anki opens, with progress. | done |
| 5 | **Your own look.** A card template in HTML and CSS with fields such as `{{term}}` and `{{image}}`, and your own CSS for the whole site. No scripts: they never run. | done |
| 6 | **Your own AI, through MCP.** Connect Claude, ChatGPT or any MCP client to `ordkort.com/mcp`: it can look words up, build a deck with pictures and a design, and give you a link that adds it to your cards. | planned |

## Later

- FSRS parameters fitted to your own answer history (the history is collected from step 1 on).
- Cloze cards and multi-field cards of your own (cloze practice from examples already exists).
- A public library of decks people share, with quality checks.
- Packs for teachers, with optional group statistics that keep learners anonymous.
- Very large collections (50,000+ cards), a desktop layout, native apps, a self-hosted mode.
- Beyond words: phrases, grammar patterns, short dialogues.

## Not doing

Accounts or required sign-up; ads or tracking; a subscription at the core; turning into a general
note-taking tool.

## How we know it works

People keep studying for 30 days and more; few reports of wrong cards; organic visits and GitHub stars grow;
people make and share their own decks; Ordkort is recommended as "Anki for languages, but easier".
