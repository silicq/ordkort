Ordkort — learn any language with flashcards

Free flashcards with spaced repetition for 60 languages: ready word lists by topic and level, a learner’s dictionary with inflection tables, a grammar book, a translator that explains every word and a reading mode. No ads, no sign-up; your cards stay in your browser. The app needs JavaScript.
