en flyplass

noun, masculine

airport

Learn “flyplass” in Ordkort

Forms

SingularPlural
Indefiniteen flyplassflyplasser
Definiteflyplassenflyplassene

In simple Norwegian

et sted der fly tar av og lander

Examples

  • Toget går rett til flyplassen.The train goes straight to the airport.
  • Vi må være på flyplassen to timer før.We have to be at the airport two hours before.
  • Hun henter meg på flyplassen.She is picking me up at the airport.
  • Gardermoen er Oslos største flyplass.Gardermoen is Oslo’s largest airport.
  • Flyplassen ligger langt fra byen.The airport is far from town.
  • Jeg jobber i en kafé på flyplassen.I work in a café at the airport.

Useful phrases

  • på flyplassen at the airport
  • til flyplassen to the airport
  • flyplassbuss airport bus
  • fra flyplassen from the airport

Good to know

Flyplass is masculine: flyplass, flyplassen, flyplasser — literally “plane place”. Official names use lufthavn: Oslo lufthavn, Gardermoen. Norwegians are på flyplassen. Norway has around fifty airports with scheduled flights because many places are hard to reach by road.
