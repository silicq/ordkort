en bedrift

noun, masculine

company, business

Learn “bedrift” in Ordkort

Forms

SingularPlural
Indefiniteen bedriftbedrifter
Definitebedriftenbedriftene

In simple Norwegian

et firma som produserer varer eller tjenester

Examples

  • Hun jobber i en stor bedrift.She works in a large company.
  • Bedriften har femti ansatte.The company has fifty employees.
  • Han startet sin egen bedrift.He started his own business.
  • Mange bedrifter mangler arbeidskraft.Many companies lack workers.
  • Bedriftshelsetjenesten tar seg av helsen til de ansatte.The occupational health service looks after employees’ health.

Useful phrases

  • liten bedrift small business
  • starte bedrift to start a business
  • bedriftshelsetjeneste occupational health service
  • i bedriften in the company

Good to know

Bedrift is feminine or masculine: bedrifta/bedriften, bedrifter. It is a general word for a company; firma means much the same, while selskap is used about legal forms like aksjeselskap (AS, limited company). Many job ads end with Vi er en bedrift i vekst — we are a growing company.

In Bokmålsordboka

  1. handels-, håndverks- eller industriforetak bedriften har 200 ansatte · være innehaver av en stor bedrift
  2. bragd, prestasjon, storverk det var en bedrift å få boka så fort ferdig · fortelle om sine bedrifter
