en prosent

noun, masculine

per cent

Learn “prosent” in Ordkort

Forms

SingularPlural
Indefiniteen prosentprosenter / prosent
Definiteprosentenprosentene

In simple Norwegian

del av hundre, skrives %

Examples

  • Prisene steg med fem prosent.Prices rose by five per cent.
  • Hun jobber i åtti prosent stilling.She works an eighty per cent position.
  • Jeg er hundre prosent enig.I agree a hundred per cent.
  • Renten er fire prosent.The interest rate is four per cent.
  • Femti prosent rabatt i dag!Fifty per cent off today!

Useful phrases

  • prosent rabatt per cent off
  • stillingsprosent job percentage (FTE)
  • prosent av per cent of
  • hundre prosent one hundred per cent

Good to know

Prosent does not change after numbers: fem prosent, femti prosent. In Norway many jobs are described by stillingsprosent: en 50 % stilling is a half-time job, which matters for income and benefits. Note that Norwegians write a space before the sign: 50 %.

In Bokmålsordboka

  1. for hvert hundre, hundredel; fork pst., symbol % banken gir 9 prosent rente pro anno
  2. antall per hundre; del en stor prosent av befolkningen

Fixed expressions

  • hundre prosent – fullt ut, helt

Compounds

dødsprosent, skatteprosent, strykprosent, strykeprosent
