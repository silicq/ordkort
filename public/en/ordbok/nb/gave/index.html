ei/en gave

noun, feminine, masculine

present, gift

Learn “gave” in Ordkort

Forms

SingularPlural
Indefiniteei/en gavegaver
Definitegava / gavengavene

In simple Norwegian

noe man gir til noen uten å ta betalt

Examples

  • Takk for gaven!Thanks for the present!
  • Hva skal vi kjøpe i gave til henne?What shall we buy her as a present?
  • Barna åpnet gavene på julaften.The children opened their presents on Christmas Eve.
  • Hun har en gave for språk.She has a gift for languages.
  • Gaven var pakket inn i rødt papir.The present was wrapped in red paper.

Useful phrases

  • i gave as a present
  • gavekort gift card
  • julegave Christmas present
  • pakke inn en gave to wrap a present

Good to know

Gave is masculine: gave, gaven, gaver. In Norway Christmas presents are opened on julaften, 24 December, in the evening. Gavekort (gift cards) are very popular. Gave also means a talent: en gave for musikk. The verb is gi.

In Bokmålsordboka

  1. noe en gir eller får uten vederlag; presang; donasjon det er en gave · få som gave
  2. anlegg, talent ha sangens gave

Fixed expressions

  • milde gaver – rause gaver (til veldedige formål)

Compounds

bryllupsgave, bursdagsgave, julegave, talegave
