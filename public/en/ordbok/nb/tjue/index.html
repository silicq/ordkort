tjue

numeral

twenty

Learn “tjue” in Ordkort

In simple Norwegian

tallet 20

Examples

  • Hun er tjue år.She is twenty.
  • Det tar tjue minutter med buss.It takes twenty minutes by bus.
  • Vi kjøpte tjuefem epler.We bought twenty-five apples.
  • Klokka er tjue over ni.It is twenty past nine.
  • Den tjuende i hver måned får jeg lønn.I get paid on the twentieth of each month.
  • Bussen går hvert tjuende minutt.The bus runs every twenty minutes.
  • Jeg trenger tjue kroner til parkering.I need twenty kroner for parking.
  • Det er tjue elever i klassen.There are twenty pupils in the class.
  • Det kommer tjue gjester i bursdagen.Twenty guests are coming to the birthday party.
  • Han har jobbet her i tjue år.He has worked here for twenty years.
  • Toget går tjue over seks.The train leaves at twenty past six.
  • Billetten koster tjue kroner for barn.The ticket costs twenty kroner for children.

Useful phrases

  • tjuende twentieth
  • tjueen twenty-one
  • en tjuekroning a twenty-krone coin
  • tjue over twenty past

Good to know

Tjue is said “kju-eh”. Since 1951 the official way to say 21–99 is “tens first”: tjueen, tjueto (twenty-one, twenty-two). Many older Norwegians still say the old way, “en-og-tjue”, like German. Both are understood. The variant spelling tyve is also allowed.
