verb

to celebrate

In simple Norwegian

å markere en høytid eller en glad begivenhet

Examples

  • Vi feirer jul hos foreldrene mine.We celebrate Christmas at my parents’.
  • Hvordan feirer dere nasjonaldagen?How do you celebrate the national day?
  • Det må vi feire!We must celebrate that!
  • Hun feiret bursdagen sin med venner.She celebrated her birthday with friends.
  • Muslimer i Norge feirer id.Muslims in Norway celebrate Eid.

Useful phrases

  • feire jul to celebrate Christmas
  • feire bursdag to celebrate a birthday
  • feiring celebration
  • 17. mai-feiring 17 May celebration

Good to know

Feire is regular: feirer, feiret, har feiret. The biggest celebrations in Norway are jul (Christmas, celebrated on the evening of 24 December) and 17. mai, Constitution Day, with parades and national costumes (bunad). Many families celebrate both Norwegian holidays and those of their own traditions.
