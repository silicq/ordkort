fire

numeral

four

Learn “fire” in Ordkort

In simple Norwegian

tallet 4

Examples

  • Vi er fire i familien.There are four of us in the family.
  • Klokka er fire.It is four o’clock.
  • Jeg har bodd her i fire år.I have lived here for four years.
  • Butikken stenger kvart over fire.The shop closes at a quarter past four.
  • Hun bor i fjerde etasje.She lives on the fourth floor.
  • Kurset varer i fire uker.The course lasts four weeks.
  • Vi var fire på hytta.There were four of us at the cabin.
  • Hun har fire barnebarn.She has four grandchildren.

Useful phrases

  • fjerde fourth
  • firkant square
  • på alle fire on all fours
  • fire ganger four times

Good to know

Fire is pronounced with a long i: “fee-reh”, and it has nothing to do with English “fire” (that is brann or ild). The ordinal is fjerde, pronounced “fyæ-reh”. Fire is also a verb meaning to lower something on a rope: fire ned en båt.
