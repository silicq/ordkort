en hage

noun, masculine

garden, yard

Learn “hage” in Ordkort

Forms

SingularPlural
Indefiniteen hagehager
Definitehagenhagene

In simple Norwegian

et område med gress, blomster eller grønnsaker ved et hus

Examples

  • Barna leker i hagen.The children are playing in the garden.
  • Vi har epletrær i hagen.We have apple trees in the garden.
  • Han jobber i hagen hver lørdag.He works in the garden every Saturday.
  • Huset har en stor hage.The house has a large garden.
  • Barnehagen ligger rett ved siden av.The kindergarten is right next door.

Useful phrases

  • i hagen in the garden
  • barnehage kindergarten
  • dyrehage zoo
  • kjøkkenhage vegetable garden

Good to know

Hage is masculine: hage, hagen, hager. Barnehage — literally “children’s garden” — is kindergarten, and dyrehage — “animals’ garden” — is a zoo. Norwegians spend a lot of the short summer in the hage; mowing the lawn on Sunday may annoy the neighbours.

In Bokmålsordboka

  1. (inngjerdet) jordstykke (ved hus, gård) der en kan dyrke frukt, bær, grønnsaker eller prydplanter en hage med mange blomster · ha en hage å stelle med
  2. inngjerdet stykke beitemark; havn, hamn

Fixed expressions

  • Edens hage – paradisisk sted

Compounds

frukthage, grønnsakhage, kjøkkenhage, havnehage, hamnehage, hestehage
