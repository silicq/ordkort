en ost

noun, masculine

cheese

In simple Norwegian

mat som lages av melk

Examples

  • Jeg spiser brød med ost.I eat bread with cheese.
  • Har du prøvd brunost?Have you tried brown cheese?
  • Osten ligger i kjøleskapet.The cheese is in the fridge.
  • Kan du skjære litt ost?Can you slice some cheese?
  • Norge har mange gode oster.Norway has many good cheeses.
  • Kan du kjøpe en pakke ost?Can you buy a pack of cheese?

Useful phrases

  • brunost brown (whey) cheese
  • ostehøvel cheese slicer
  • skive ost slice of cheese
  • revet ost grated cheese

Good to know

Ost is masculine: ost, osten, oster. Brunost — sweet, brown cheese made from whey — is a Norwegian speciality, eaten in thin slices cut with an ostehøvel (cheese slicer), a Norwegian invention from 1925. Soft fresh cheese is not ost but kesam or cottage cheese.
