en rom

noun, masculine

room; space

Learn “rom” in Ordkort

Forms

SingularPlural
Indefiniteen romrommer
Definiterommenrommene

In simple Norwegian

en del av et hus med vegger og dør; også verdensrommet

Examples

  • Leiligheten har tre rom og kjøkken.The flat has three rooms and a kitchen.
  • Barna har hvert sitt rom.The children each have their own room.
  • Jeg leier et rom hos en familie.I rent a room from a family.
  • Hotellet har ledige rom.The hotel has vacant rooms.
  • Hun drømmer om å reise ut i rommet.She dreams of travelling into space.

Useful phrases

  • soverom bedroom
  • stue og to rom a living room and two rooms
  • ledig rom vacant room
  • rom til leie room to let

Good to know

Rom is neuter and unchanged in the plural: et rom, to rom; rommet, rommene. Norwegian flat ads count rooms differently from many countries: a treroms leilighet has a living room and two bedrooms, the kitchen not counted. A hybel is a small rented room, often for students. Rom also means outer space.
