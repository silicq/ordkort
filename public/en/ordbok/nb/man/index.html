man

pronoun

one, you, people (in general)

Learn “man” in Ordkort

In simple Norwegian

et pronomen som betyr folk flest eller hvem som helst, ikke en bestemt person

Examples

  • Her må man ta av seg skoene.Here you have to take off your shoes.
  • Man sier at vinteren blir kald i år.They say the winter will be cold this year.
  • Hvordan sier man «thank you» på norsk?How do you say “thank you” in Norwegian?
  • Man vet aldri hva som kan skje.You never know what might happen.
  • I Norge spiser man middag tidlig.In Norway people eat dinner early.

Useful phrases

  • man må one must, you have to
  • man kan one can, it is possible
  • man skulle tro you would think
  • som man sier as they say

Good to know

Man is only a subject; as an object Norwegians use en (Det gjør en glad) and the possessive is sin. It is common in rules and instructions: Man må ha billett. Russian and Ukrainian usually translate it with an impersonal verb (говорят, нужно), English with “you” or “people”.

In Bokmålsordboka

  1. bare som subjekt: en, folk man kan aldri vite · man har ikke godt av nikotin
