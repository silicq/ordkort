en student

noun, masculine

(university) student

Learn “student” in Ordkort

Forms

SingularPlural
Indefiniteen studentstudenter
Definitestudentenstudentene

In simple Norwegian

en person som studerer ved en høgskole eller et universitet

Examples

  • Hun er student ved universitetet i Bergen.She is a student at the University of Bergen.
  • Studentene har eksamen i mai.The students have exams in May.
  • Som student får du rabatt på bussen.As a student you get a discount on the bus.
  • Mange studenter bor på hybel.Many students live in bedsits.
  • Han jobber deltid ved siden av studiene.He works part-time alongside his studies.

Useful phrases

  • studentrabatt student discount
  • studentby student village
  • student ved student at
  • utvekslingsstudent exchange student

Good to know

Student is masculine: student, studenten, studenter. In Norway it means someone at university or college; school pupils are elever. Students get loans and grants from Lånekassen. Russ — the final-year school pupils in red overalls in May — are not called studenter.

  1. person som studerer ved universitet eller høyskole holde foredrag for studenter
