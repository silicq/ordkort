en politi

noun, masculine

police

In simple Norwegian

de som skal passe på lov og orden

Examples

  • Ring politiet på 112!Call the police on 112!
  • Politiet kom raskt.The police came quickly.
  • Du må søke om pass hos politiet.You apply for a passport at the police.
  • Han jobber i politiet.He works in the police.
  • Politiet ber om tips.The police are asking for tips.
  • Politiet ber folk holde seg hjemme.The police are asking people to stay at home.
  • Hun ringte politiet da hun så innbruddet.She called the police when she saw the break-in.

Useful phrases

  • politistasjon police station
  • politibetjent police officer
  • melde til politiet to report to the police
  • ringe politiet to call the police

Good to know

Politi is neuter and usually definite: politiet. One officer is en politibetjent or simply en politi in speech. The emergency number is 112; for non-urgent matters call 02800. In Norway the police also issue passports and handle many residence permit appointments.
