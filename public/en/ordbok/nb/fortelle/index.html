å fortelle

verb

to tell

In simple Norwegian

å si noe til noen, ofte en historie

Examples

  • Fortell meg om familien din.Tell me about your family.
  • Hun fortalte en morsom historie.She told a funny story.
  • Han fortalte at han skulle flytte.He said that he was going to move.
  • Ikke fortell det til noen!Don’t tell anyone!
  • Kan du fortelle meg veien?Can you tell me the way?

Useful phrases

  • fortelle om to tell about
  • fortelle en historie to tell a story
  • fortelling story, narrative
  • fortelle videre to pass on

Good to know

Fortelle is irregular: forteller, fortalte, har fortalt. The listener follows directly: fortelle meg, fortelle barna. Unlike si (say), fortelle is used for longer stories and news: Fortell om deg selv — a common task in the oral part of the norskprøve.
