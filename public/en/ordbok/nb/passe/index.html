å passe

verb

to fit, to suit; to look after

Learn “passe” in Ordkort

Forms

Infinitiveå passe
Presentpasser
Pastpassa / passet
Present perfecthar passa / passet
Imperativepass!
Passivepasses

In simple Norwegian

å ha riktig størrelse, å være praktisk; også å ta vare på

Examples

  • Passer skoene?Do the shoes fit?
  • Passer det med tirsdag?Does Tuesday suit you?
  • Kan du passe barna i kveld?Can you look after the children tonight?
  • Pass deg for bilen!Watch out for the car!
  • Det passer bra.That suits me fine.
  • Kjolen passer perfekt.The dress fits perfectly.
  • Passer det at jeg kommer klokka fem?Is it all right if I come at five?
  • Han passer hunden når vi er bortreist.He looks after the dog when we are away.

Useful phrases

  • passe på to look after, to watch
  • pass deg! watch out!
  • passe sammen to match
  • det passer it suits

Good to know

Passe has many uses: clothes fit (Jakka passer), times suit (Passer det?), and you look after someone (passe barna, passe på). Pass deg! — watch out! Past: passet, har passet. Passende means appropriate.
