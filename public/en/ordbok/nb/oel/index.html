en øl

noun, masculine

beer

Learn “øl” in Ordkort

Forms

SingularPlural
Indefiniteen øløler
Definiteølenølene

In simple Norwegian

en drikk med alkohol laget av korn og humle

Examples

  • Skal vi ta en øl?Shall we have a beer?
  • Øl selges i butikken til klokka åtte.Beer is sold in shops until eight.
  • To øl, takk!Two beers, please!
  • Han drikker alkoholfritt øl.He drinks alcohol-free beer.
  • Ølet er dyrt i Norge.Beer is expensive in Norway.
  • Han bestilte et glass øl og en pizza.He ordered a glass of beer and a pizza.
  • Øl er dyrt på restaurant.Beer is expensive in restaurants.

Useful phrases

  • en øl a (glass of) beer
  • alkoholfritt øl alcohol-free beer
  • juleøl Christmas beer
  • ta en øl to grab a beer

Good to know

Øl is neuter as a substance (ølet), but en øl means a glass or bottle: Skal vi ta en øl? Supermarkets sell beer only until 8 pm on weekdays and 6 pm on Saturdays, never on Sundays; stronger drinks are sold only at Vinmonopolet. You must be 18 to buy beer.
