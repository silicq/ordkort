en restaurant

noun, masculine

restaurant

Learn “restaurant” in Ordkort

Forms

SingularPlural
Indefiniteen restaurantrestauranter
Definiterestaurantenrestaurantene

In simple Norwegian

et sted der man betaler for å spise

Examples

  • Skal vi gå på restaurant i kveld?Shall we go to a restaurant tonight?
  • Restauranten har gode anmeldelser.The restaurant has good reviews.
  • Han jobber som kokk på en restaurant.He works as a chef at a restaurant.
  • Vi har bestilt bord på restauranten.We have booked a table at the restaurant.
  • Det er dyrt å spise på restaurant i Norge.It is expensive to eat out in Norway.
  • Restauranten er stengt på mandager.The restaurant is closed on Mondays.

Useful phrases

  • gå på restaurant to go to a restaurant
  • på restauranten at the restaurant
  • restaurantbransjen the restaurant industry
  • en god restaurant a good restaurant

Good to know

Restaurant is masculine: restaurant, restauranten, restauranter. Many Norwegians say it “restau-RANG”, with the stress on the last syllable. Norwegians go på restaurant. Tipping is not expected, as service is included in the price, though rounding up is common.
