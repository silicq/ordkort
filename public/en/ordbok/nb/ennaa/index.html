ennå

adverb

yet, still

Learn “ennå” in Ordkort

In simple Norwegian

fram til nå; fortsatt

Examples

  • Han har ikke kommet ennå.He hasn’t come yet.
  • Er du ikke ferdig ennå?Aren’t you finished yet?
  • Det regner ennå.It is still raining.
  • Jeg vet ikke ennå om jeg kommer.I don’t know yet if I will come.
  • Ennå er det tid.There is still time.
  • Har du fått svar ennå?Have you had an answer yet?
  • Barna sover ikke ennå.The children aren’t asleep yet.
  • Vi har ikke spist middag ennå.We haven’t had dinner yet.

Useful phrases

  • ikke ennå not yet
  • ennå ikke not yet
  • ennå en gang once again
  • har ennå ikke has not yet

Good to know

Ennå is mostly used with negation: ikke ennå — not yet. In positive sentences fortsatt is more common for “still”: Hun bor fortsatt her. The stress is on the second syllable: en-NÅ. Ikke ennå is a complete answer to a question: Har du spist? – Ikke ennå.
