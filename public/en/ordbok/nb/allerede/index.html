allerede

adverb

already

Learn “allerede” in Ordkort

In simple Norwegian

tidligere enn man kanskje trodde

Examples

  • Er du ferdig allerede?Are you finished already?
  • Jeg har allerede spist.I have already eaten.
  • Det er allerede mørkt klokka fire.It is already dark at four.
  • Allerede som barn ville hun bli lege.Already as a child she wanted to be a doctor.
  • Har du allerede kjøpt billetter?Have you already bought tickets?

Useful phrases

  • allerede nå already now
  • allerede i morgen as early as tomorrow
  • har allerede have already
  • allerede da even then

Good to know

Allerede stands in the same place as ikke: Jeg har allerede spist. In speech, Norwegians often use alt instead: Er du ferdig alt? In questions, ennå often replaces it for “yet”: Har du spist ennå? — Have you eaten yet?
