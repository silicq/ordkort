en sang

noun, masculine

song; sang (past of synge)

Learn “sang” in Ordkort

Forms

SingularPlural
Indefiniteen sangsanger
Definitesangensangene

In simple Norwegian

en tekst med melodi; også preteritum av synge

Examples

  • Dette er min favorittsang.This is my favourite song.
  • Vi sang bursdagssangen for henne.We sang the birthday song for her.
  • Sangen handler om kjærlighet.The song is about love.
  • Kan du teksten til sangen?Do you know the lyrics of the song?
  • Barna sang hele veien hjem.The children sang all the way home.

Useful phrases

  • synge en sang to sing a song
  • nasjonalsang national anthem
  • sangtekst lyrics
  • bursdagssang birthday song

Good to know

Sang is a masculine noun (sang, sangen, sanger) and also the past tense of synge: Hun sang. Norway’s national anthem, Ja, vi elsker, is a sang everyone sings on 17 May. Song lyrics are sangtekst. Reading lyrics while listening is a popular way to learn Norwegian.
