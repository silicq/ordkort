ni

numeral

nine

Learn “ni” in Ordkort

In simple Norwegian

tallet 9

Examples

  • Butikken åpner klokka ni.The shop opens at nine.
  • Hun går i niende klasse.She is in ninth grade.
  • Det er ni grader ute.It is nine degrees outside.
  • Toget har ni vogner.The train has nine carriages.
  • Vi var ni personer på middag.We were nine at dinner.
  • Vi har ni elever i gruppa.We have nine pupils in the group.
  • Kurset varer i ni måneder.The course lasts nine months.
  • Hun bor i niende etasje i en høy blokk.She lives on the ninth floor of a tall block.
  • Jeg legger meg klokka ni om kvelden.I go to bed at nine in the evening.
  • Toget går ni minutter over sju.The train leaves at nine minutes past seven.
  • Hun har bodd i Norge i ni år.She has lived in Norway for nine years.
  • Vi kjøpte ni boller til barna.We bought nine buns for the children.

Useful phrases

  • niende ninth
  • nitti ninety
  • nitten nineteen
  • halv ni half past eight

Good to know

Ni simply means nine. Related forms: nitten (19), nitti (90), niende (ninth). Niende klasse is the second-to-last year of lower secondary school, when pupils are 14–15.
