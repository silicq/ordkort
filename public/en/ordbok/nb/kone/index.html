en/ei kone

noun, masculine, feminine

wife

Learn “kone” in Ordkort

Forms

SingularPlural
Indefiniteen/ei konekoner
Definitekonen / konakonene

In simple Norwegian

en gift kvinne sett fra mannen hennes

Examples

  • Dette er kona mi, Anna.This is my wife, Anna.
  • Han og kona har tre barn.He and his wife have three children.
  • Kona hans jobber på sykehuset.His wife works at the hospital.
  • De ble mann og kone i fjor.They became husband and wife last year.
  • Hilsen til kona!Say hello to your wife!

Useful phrases

  • kona mi my wife
  • mann og kone husband and wife
  • ektefelle spouse
  • samboer partner (living together)

Good to know

Kone can be feminine (kona) or masculine (konen); kona mi is the most common in speech. The neutral official word is ektefelle (spouse). Many couples in Norway live together without marrying — then the partner is samboer, a status with its own rules for tax and benefits.

In Bokmålsordboka

  1. kvinnelig ektefelle; gift kvinne ha kone og barn · mann og kone
  2. eldre kvinne en gammel kone
