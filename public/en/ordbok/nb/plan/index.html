en plan

noun, masculine

plan

The Norwegian noun plan means “plan”. Definite: planen; plural: planer, planene.

Learn “plan” in Ordkort

Forms

SingularPlural
Indefiniteen planplaner
Definiteplanenplanene

In simple Norwegian

en oversikt over hva man skal gjøre

Examples

  • Hva er planen for helgen?What is the plan for the weekend?
  • Vi har ingen planer i kveld.We have no plans tonight.
  • Alt gikk etter planen.Everything went according to plan.
  • Har du en plan B?Do you have a plan B?
  • De planlegger å flytte til Bergen.They are planning to move to Bergen.

Useful phrases

  • etter planen according to plan
  • planlegge to plan
  • ha planer om to plan to
  • ukeplan weekly plan

Good to know

Plan is masculine: plan, planen, planer. The verb is planlegge (literally “lay a plan”): planlegger, planla, har planlagt. Schools send pupils an ukeplan — a weekly plan with homework — which parents are expected to follow. Plan is also a level or floor: på plan 2.

In Bokmålsordboka

  1. grunnriss eller kart over et område som skal bygges ut
  2. utkast, forslag for hvordan en skal gjennomføre noe huset ble bygd etter planen
  3. tanke, hensikt hvilke planer har du for framtiden?
