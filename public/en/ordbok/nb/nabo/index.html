en nabo

noun, masculine

neighbour

Learn “nabo” in Ordkort

Forms

SingularPlural
Indefiniteen nabonaboer
Definitenaboennaboene

In simple Norwegian

en som bor i nærheten

Examples

  • Naboen vår er veldig hyggelig.Our neighbour is very nice.
  • Vi har nye naboer.We have new neighbours.
  • Kan du låne meg en kopp sukker, nabo?Could you lend me a cup of sugar, neighbour?
  • Naboene klaget på bråket.The neighbours complained about the noise.
  • Sverige er Norges nabo i øst.Sweden is Norway’s neighbour to the east.
  • Vi spiste grillmat med naboene i hagen.We had a barbecue with the neighbours in the garden.

Useful phrases

  • naboen min my neighbour
  • nabolag neighbourhood
  • nabokjerring nosy neighbour
  • nabolandet the neighbouring country

Good to know

Nabo is masculine: nabo, naboen, naboer. Norwegians often keep a polite distance from neighbours, but a dugnad — a shared work day in the housing cooperative — is a good chance to get to know them. Quiet hours (usually from 23:00) are written in the house rules (husordensregler).

In Bokmålsordboka

  1. person som bor nær ved; granne ha gode naboer
  2. naboland Sverige er vår nabo i øst
