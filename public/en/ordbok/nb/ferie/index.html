en ferie

noun, masculine

holiday, vacation

Learn “ferie” in Ordkort

Forms

SingularPlural
Indefiniteen ferieferier
Definiteferienferiene

In simple Norwegian

en tid da man har fri fra jobb eller skole

Examples

  • Vi har ferie i juli.We are on holiday in July.
  • God ferie!Have a good holiday!
  • Hvor skal dere på ferie?Where are you going on holiday?
  • Barna har høstferie neste uke.The children have autumn break next week.
  • Alle har rett på fem uker ferie.Everyone is entitled to five weeks’ holiday.

Useful phrases

  • på ferie on holiday
  • feriepenger holiday pay
  • sommerferie summer holiday
  • ta ferie to take time off

Good to know

Ferie is masculine: ferie, ferien, ferier. Employees in Norway have the right to five weeks of ferie, and instead of normal pay in June most receive feriepenger — holiday pay saved up the year before. Fellesferie, in July, is when much of the country shuts down.

Compounds

fellesferie, juleferie, skoleferie, sommerferie, badeferie
