å fortsette

verb

to continue

In simple Norwegian

å holde fram med noe

Examples

  • Vi fortsetter i morgen.We will continue tomorrow.
  • Fortsett rett fram til lyskrysset.Continue straight ahead to the traffic lights.
  • Hun fortsatte å jobbe etter at hun fikk barn.She kept working after she had a child.
  • Regnet fortsatte hele dagen.The rain went on all day.
  • Kan vi fortsette samtalen senere?Can we continue the conversation later?

Useful phrases

  • fortsette med to go on with
  • fortsette å to keep doing
  • fortsettelse continuation
  • fortsatt still

Good to know

Fortsette is irregular like sette: fortsetter, fortsatte, har fortsatt. The perfect participle fortsatt is also an everyday adverb meaning “still”: Han bor fortsatt hjemme. Before a verb add å: fortsette å lese. Fortsettelse følger — “to be continued”.
