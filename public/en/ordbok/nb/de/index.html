de

pronoun

they; the (before an adjective)

Learn “de” in Ordkort

Forms

Formsde, dem

In simple Norwegian

ordet for flere personer eller ting vi snakker om

Examples

  • De bor i en stor leilighet.They live in a big flat.
  • Jeg har ikke sett dem i dag.I haven’t seen them today.
  • De nye naboene er hyggelige.The new neighbours are nice.
  • Hvor er barna? De leker ute.Where are the children? They are playing outside.
  • Vi spiste middag med dem.We had dinner with them.

Useful phrases

  • de andre the others
  • de fleste most (people)
  • alle sammen all of them
  • De you (old polite form)

Good to know

De is pronounced “di”, and its object form dem is pronounced “dem”. Learners often write dem as a subject: it is De bor her, not “Dem bor her”. Before an adjective de is the plural definite article: de store husene. The polite capital De has almost vanished from everyday Norwegian.
