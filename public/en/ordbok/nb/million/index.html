In simple Norwegian

tusen ganger tusen; tallet 1 000 000

Examples

  • Norge har over fem millioner innbyggere.Norway has over five million inhabitants.
  • Leiligheten kostet fire millioner kroner.The flat cost four million kroner.
  • Han vant en million i Lotto.He won a million in the lottery.
  • Takk en million!Thanks a million!
  • Millioner av mennesker så åpningen.Millions of people watched the opening.
  • Prosjektet koster flere millioner.The project costs several million.
  • Over en million turister besøker Norge hvert år.Over a million tourists visit Norway every year.

Useful phrases

  • millioner av millions of
  • en halv million half a million
  • millionær millionaire
  • i millioner in millions

Good to know

Unlike hundre and tusen, million is a real noun with an article and plural: en million, to millioner. Large prices are often shortened in speech: Leiligheten koster fire komma fem — 4.5 million. In news, mill. or mill. kr is a common abbreviation; milliard means a billion (1 000 000 000).
