å arbeide

verb

to work

In simple Norwegian

å gjøre en innsats for å få noe gjort, ofte som yrke

Examples

  • Han arbeider i kommunen.He works for the municipality.
  • Vi arbeider for et bedre miljø.We work for a better environment.
  • Hun har arbeidet her siden 2015.She has worked here since 2015.
  • Forskerne arbeider med en ny vaksine.The researchers are working on a new vaccine.
  • Man må ha arbeidstillatelse for å arbeide i Norge.You need a work permit to work in Norway.

Useful phrases

  • arbeide med to work on/with
  • arbeide for to work for (a cause)
  • arbeidstillatelse work permit
  • arbeidsgiver employer

Good to know

Arbeide is the formal twin of jobbe and is the word you meet in contracts, job ads and on NAV’s pages. It is the root of many official words: arbeidsgiver (employer), arbeidstaker (employee), arbeidsledig (unemployed), arbeidstillatelse (work permit). The noun is arbeid. Past: arbeidet, har arbeidet.
