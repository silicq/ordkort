rask

adjective

fast, quick

Learn “rask” in Ordkort

Neuterraskt

In simple Norwegian

som går fort

Examples

  • Hun er en rask løper.She is a fast runner.
  • Takk for raskt svar!Thanks for the quick reply!
  • Han lærte norsk veldig raskt.He learned Norwegian very quickly.
  • Vi tok en rask kaffe før møtet.We had a quick coffee before the meeting.
  • Toget er raskere enn bussen.The train is faster than the bus.

Useful phrases

  • raskt svar quick reply
  • så raskt som mulig as quickly as possible
  • raskere faster
  • rask og effektiv quick and efficient

Good to know

Rask compares regularly: rask – raskere – raskest. The neuter raskt is the adverb: Han lærte raskt. Fort means the same and is more common in speech: Kjør ikke så fort! Rask can also mean healthy, as in frisk og rask — fit and well.

In Bokmålsordboka

  1. hurtig, kvikk en rask hest · være rask til beins
  2. sunn, frisk rask og rørig · være frisk og rask

Fixed expressions

  • være rask på labben – reagere hurtig
