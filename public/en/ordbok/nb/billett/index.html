en billett

noun, masculine

ticket

Learn “billett” in Ordkort

Forms

SingularPlural
Indefiniteen billettbilletter
Definitebillettenbillettene

In simple Norwegian

et bevis på at man har betalt for en reise eller et arrangement

Examples

  • Har du kjøpt billett?Have you bought a ticket?
  • Billetten kan kjøpes i appen.The ticket can be bought in the app.
  • Vi har billetter til konserten.We have tickets for the concert.
  • Uten billett får du bot.Without a ticket you get a fine.
  • En enkeltbillett til sentrum, takk.A single ticket to the centre, please.

Useful phrases

  • kjøpe billett to buy a ticket
  • enkeltbillett single ticket
  • periodebillett season ticket
  • billett til ticket for

Good to know

Billett is masculine with double l and double t: billett, billetten, billetter. On buses and trams you must have a valid ticket before boarding, usually bought in an app such as Ruter or Entur; inspectors give a large fine (bot) without one. A monthly pass is periodebillett or månedskort.

In Bokmålsordboka

  1. kort eller lignende som gir adgang til offentlige transportmidler, forskjellige typer forestillinger og annet mangle billett · ha gyldig billett
