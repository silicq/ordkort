en buss

noun, masculine

bus

Learn “buss” in Ordkort

Forms

SingularPlural
Indefiniteen bussbusser
Definitebussenbussene

In simple Norwegian

et stort kjøretøy som frakter mange passasjerer

Examples

  • Jeg tar bussen til skolen.I take the bus to school.
  • Når går neste buss?When does the next bus leave?
  • Bussen var full i dag.The bus was full today.
  • Du må gå av bussen ved sykehuset.You have to get off the bus at the hospital.
  • Har du kjøpt billett til bussen?Have you bought a ticket for the bus?
  • Jeg mistet bussen og kom for sent.I missed the bus and was late.

Useful phrases

  • ta bussen to take the bus
  • gå av bussen to get off the bus
  • med buss by bus
  • busstopp bus stop

Good to know

Buss is masculine with a double s: buss, bussen, busser, bussene. You go på bussen (get on) and av bussen (get off). Tickets are usually bought in an app before boarding; the driver often does not sell them. Busstopp or holdeplass is the bus stop.
