en jobb

noun, masculine

job, work

Learn “jobb” in Ordkort

Forms

SingularPlural
Indefiniteen jobbjobber
Definitejobbenjobbene

In simple Norwegian

arbeidet man får lønn for, eller arbeidsplassen

Examples

  • Jeg har fått ny jobb!I have got a new job!
  • Hun er på jobb nå.She is at work now.
  • Det er vanskelig å finne jobb uten norsk.It is hard to find a job without Norwegian.
  • Jeg søker jobb som sykepleier.I am applying for jobs as a nurse.
  • Godt jobbet!Well done!

Useful phrases

  • på jobb at work
  • søke jobb to apply for jobs
  • deltidsjobb part-time job
  • godt jobbet well done

Good to know

Jobb is masculine: jobb, jobben, jobber, jobbene. You are på jobb (at work) and go til jobben. Søke jobb is to apply for jobs: you send a søknad and a CV. Godt jobbet! — “well done!” — is a common compliment at school and work. Arbeid means the same but is more formal.
