en/ei fly

noun, masculine, feminine

plane; to fly

Learn “fly” in Ordkort

Forms

SingularPlural
Indefiniteen/ei flyflyer
Definiteflyen / flyaflyene

In simple Norwegian

et luftfartøy med vinger; også å bevege seg gjennom lufta

Examples

  • Flyet til Tromsø er forsinket.The plane to Tromsø is delayed.
  • Vi flyr til Spania i morgen.We are flying to Spain tomorrow.
  • Fuglene fløy sørover.The birds flew south.
  • Det er raskere å ta fly enn tog.It is quicker to fly than to take the train.
  • Tiden flyr!Time flies!
  • Flyet landet på Gardermoen klokka ti.The plane landed at Gardermoen at ten.

Useful phrases

  • ta fly to fly, to go by plane
  • flyplass airport
  • flybillett plane ticket
  • fly av gårde to rush off

Good to know

Fly is both a neuter noun and a verb. The noun: et fly, flyet, fly, flyene. The verb is irregular: flyr, fløy, har fløyet. Norway relies on domestic flights because of its long distances, so ta fly is as ordinary as taking a bus. The airport is flyplass or lufthavn.
