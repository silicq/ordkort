to

numeral

two

Learn “to” in Ordkort

In simple Norwegian

tallet 2

Examples

  • Jeg har to barn.I have two children.
  • Klokka er to.It is two o’clock.
  • Vi to må snakke sammen.The two of us need to talk.
  • Vi har to katter og en hund.We have two cats and a dog.
  • Jeg har bodd i Norge i to år.I have lived in Norway for two years.
  • Møtet varer i to timer.The meeting lasts two hours.

Useful phrases

  • begge to both
  • to ganger twice
  • annenhver every other

Good to know

Norwegian numbers have no gender after 1: to biler, to jenter, to hus. Begge to — both — is how Norwegians usually say “both of them”. The ordinal is annen or andre (second).
