In simple Norwegian

å vise glede med munnen

Examples

  • Hun smilte til meg.She smiled at me.
  • Smil til kameraet!Smile for the camera!
  • Han smiler alltid.He always smiles.
  • Barnet smilte for første gang.The baby smiled for the first time.
  • Det er lett å smile når sola skinner.It is easy to smile when the sun is shining.

Useful phrases

  • smile til to smile at
  • et smil a smile
  • smile bredt to smile broadly

Good to know

Smile is regular: smiler, smilte, har smilt. You smile til someone. The noun is et smil. Norwegians do not usually smile at strangers in the street — it is not rudeness, just a different habit.
