seg

pronoun

himself, herself, itself, themselves

Learn “seg” in Ordkort

In simple Norwegian

refleksivt pronomen som viser tilbake til subjektet i tredje person

Examples

  • Han vasker seg.He is washing (himself).
  • De gleder seg til ferien.They are looking forward to the holiday.
  • Hun satte seg ved vinduet.She sat down by the window.
  • Barna kledde på seg.The children got dressed.
  • Han føler seg bedre nå.He feels better now.

Useful phrases

  • glede seg til to look forward to
  • sette seg to sit down
  • skynde seg to hurry
  • legge seg to go to bed

Good to know

Seg is only for he, she, it, one and they; the other persons use their own object forms: jeg vasker meg, du vasker deg, vi vasker oss. Many verbs that are reflexive in Norwegian are not in English: glede seg, skynde seg, gifte seg (to get married).
