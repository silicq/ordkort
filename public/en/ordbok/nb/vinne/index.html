å vinne

verb

to win

Learn “vinne” in Ordkort

Forms

Presentvinner
Pastvinte
Present perfecthar vint
Imperativevinn!

In simple Norwegian

å bli den beste i en konkurranse eller kamp

Examples

  • Hvem vant kampen?Who won the match?
  • Norge vant fem gull i OL.Norway won five golds at the Olympics.
  • Hun vant en tur til Paris.She won a trip to Paris.
  • Vi vant to–null.We won two–nil.
  • Det er ikke det å vinne som er viktigst.Winning is not what matters most.
  • Hvem tror du vinner valget?Who do you think will win the election?

Useful phrases

  • vinne over to beat
  • vinne i lotto to win the lottery
  • vinner winner
  • vinne tid to gain time

Good to know

Vinne is irregular: vinner, vant, har vunnet. The opponent you beat takes over: Norge vant over Sverige. The opposite is tape (to lose a game) — not miste, which is for losing things. En vinner is a winner. Norwegians love winter sports, so you will hear vant gull in the news every winter.
