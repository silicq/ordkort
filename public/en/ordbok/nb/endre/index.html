å endre

verb

to change

Learn “endre” in Ordkort

In simple Norwegian

å gjøre noe annerledes

Examples

  • Planene er endret.The plans have been changed.
  • Vi må endre passordet.We have to change the password.
  • Byen har endret seg mye.The town has changed a lot.
  • Kan jeg endre bestillingen min?Can I change my booking?
  • Klimaet endrer seg.The climate is changing.
  • Kan vi endre tidspunktet for møtet?Can we change the time of the meeting?

Useful phrases

  • endre seg to change (oneself)
  • endre på to alter something
  • endring change
  • klimaendringer climate change

Good to know

Endre is used for changing something; endre seg when something changes by itself: Byen har endret seg. Forandre means the same and is a bit more spoken; bytte is for exchanging one thing for another (bytte jobb, bytte tog). Past: endret, har endret.
