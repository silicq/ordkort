du

pronoun

you (one person)

Learn “du” in Ordkort

Forms

Formsdu, deg

In simple Norwegian

ordet vi bruker når vi snakker direkte til én person

Examples

  • Hvordan har du det?How are you?
  • Du må komme og besøke oss!You must come and visit us!
  • Jeg savner deg.I miss you.
  • Kan jeg spørre deg om noe?Can I ask you something?
  • Du vet aldri.You never know.

Useful phrases

  • du og jeg you and I
  • takk til deg thank you (to you)
  • hos deg at your place
  • deg selv yourself

Good to know

Norwegians say du to almost everyone — colleagues, doctors, the boss, strangers in a shop. The formal De is rare today and may even sound cold. The object form is deg, the possessive din, ditt, dine; when speaking to several people, use dere.
