redd

adjective

afraid, scared

Learn “redd” in Ordkort

Forms

Masculine / feminineredd
Neuterredd
Definiteredde
Pluralredde
Comparativereddere
Superlativereddest
Superlative, definitereddeste

In simple Norwegian

som føler frykt

Examples

  • Jeg er redd for hunder.I am afraid of dogs.
  • Ikke vær redd!Don’t be afraid!
  • Hun var redd for å snakke norsk.She was afraid to speak Norwegian.
  • Jeg er redd det er for sent.I’m afraid it is too late.
  • Barnet ble redd av lyden.The child was frightened by the noise.
  • Jeg var redd for å gjøre feil.I was afraid of making mistakes.

Useful phrases

  • redd for afraid of
  • bli redd to get scared
  • jeg er redd at I’m afraid that
  • livredd terrified

Good to know

Norwegian has no verb “to fear” in everyday speech — instead være redd for: Jeg er redd for høyder. The neuter is redd, plural redde. Jeg er redd at … softens bad news, like English “I’m afraid”. Don’t mix it up with redde (to rescue).
