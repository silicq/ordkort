en/ei mulighet

noun, masculine, feminine

possibility, opportunity

Learn “mulighet” in Ordkort

Forms

SingularPlural
Indefiniteen/ei mulighetmuligheter
Definitemuligheten / mulighetamulighetene

In simple Norwegian

en sjanse til å gjøre noe

Examples

  • Er det mulighet for å parkere her?Is there a possibility to park here?
  • Det er mange muligheter i Norge.There are many opportunities in Norway.
  • Hun fikk muligheten til å studere.She got the opportunity to study.
  • Vi har to muligheter.We have two options.
  • Ta kontakt så snart du har mulighet.Get in touch as soon as you can.

Useful phrases

  • mulighet til opportunity to
  • ha mulighet to be able to
  • like muligheter equal opportunities
  • muligheten for the chance of

Good to know

Mulighet is feminine or masculine: muligheten/muligheta, muligheter. Mulighet til å + infinitive: muligheten til å jobbe. Ha mulighet means “to be able to, to have the chance”: Har du mulighet i morgen? — Are you available tomorrow? Like other words in -het, the stress is before the suffix.
