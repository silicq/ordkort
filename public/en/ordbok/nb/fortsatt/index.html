fortsatt

adverb

still

Learn “fortsatt” in Ordkort

Forms

Masculine / femininefortsatt
Neuterfortsatt
Definitefortsatte
Pluralfortsatte

In simple Norwegian

som før, uten at noe har endret seg

Examples

  • Bor du fortsatt i Bergen?Do you still live in Bergen?
  • Det regner fortsatt.It is still raining.
  • Jeg er fortsatt sulten.I am still hungry.
  • Hun jobber fortsatt i barnehagen.She still works at the kindergarten.
  • Fortsatt god helg!Enjoy the rest of the weekend!
  • Vi er fortsatt venner.We are still friends.

Useful phrases

  • fortsatt god dag have a nice rest of the day
  • fortsatt ikke still not
  • fortsatt god helg enjoy the rest of the weekend
  • fortsatt like still the same

Good to know

Fortsatt is the past participle of fortsette (continue) used as an adverb “still”. It stands where ikke stands: Jeg er fortsatt sulten. Fortsatt god dag! — wishing someone a good rest of the day — is a typical friendly goodbye in shops. Still also translates as stadig or fremdeles.

In Bokmålsordboka

  1. som fortsetter; videre vi håper på et fortsatt godt samarbeid
  2. brukt som adverb: fremdeles det snør fortsatt · sjokolade er fortsatt min favoritt
