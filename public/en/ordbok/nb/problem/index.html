et problem

noun, neuter

problem

Learn “problem” in Ordkort

Forms

SingularPlural

Definiteproblemetproblema / problemene

In simple Norwegian

noe som er vanskelig og må løses

Examples

  • Det er ikke noe problem.It’s no problem.
  • Vi har et problem med vasken.We have a problem with the sink.
  • Hun løste problemet raskt.She solved the problem quickly.
  • Har du problemer med å sove?Do you have trouble sleeping?
  • Problemet er at vi ikke har tid.The problem is that we don’t have time.

Useful phrases

  • ikke noe problem no problem
  • løse et problem to solve a problem
  • problemer med trouble with
  • problemfri trouble-free

Good to know

Problem is neuter: et problem, problemet, problemer, problemene. Stress falls on the last syllable: pro-BLEM. Ikke noe problem is the casual answer to a request or a thank-you. Norwegians løse (solve) a problem: løse problemet.
