jeg

pronoun

I

Learn “jeg” in Ordkort

Forms

Formsjeg, meg

In simple Norwegian

ordet den som snakker, bruker om seg selv

Examples

  • Jeg heter Anna.My name is Anna.
  • Jeg kommer fra Ukraina.I come from Ukraine.
  • Kan du hjelpe meg?Can you help me?
  • Han ringte meg i går.He called me yesterday.
  • I morgen skal jeg jobbe.Tomorrow I am going to work.
  • Jeg har lært norsk i ett år.I have studied Norwegian for one year.
  • Det var meg som ringte.It was me who called.

Useful phrases

  • jeg også me too
  • ikke jeg not me
  • meg selv myself
  • unnskyld meg excuse me

Good to know

Jeg changes to meg as an object: Han ser meg. Norwegian has only two forms, so there is no difference between “me” and “to me”: Gi meg boka — give me the book. Jeg is written with a small letter, unlike English “I”. When a sentence starts with a time or place, jeg moves after the verb: I morgen skal jeg …
