det

pronoun

it, that; there (is)

Learn “det” in Ordkort

In simple Norwegian

et pronomen for et intetkjønnsord, eller et tomt subjekt i setninger om vær, tid og lignende

Examples

  • Det regner i dag.It is raining today.
  • Det er mange folk på bussen.There are a lot of people on the bus.
  • Huset er gammelt, men det er fint.The house is old, but it is nice.
  • Det tar tid å lære et nytt språk.It takes time to learn a new language.

Useful phrases

  • det er there is, it is
  • det går bra it’s going fine
  • det spiller ingen rolle it doesn’t matter

Good to know

A Norwegian sentence always needs a subject, so det fills the gap where English has “it” or “there”: Det regner, Det er kaldt, Det står en mann der. It also replaces neuter nouns: et hus → det.
