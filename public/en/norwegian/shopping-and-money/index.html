Shopping & money in Norwegian

Shopping in Norway is mostly cashless: you pay by card or with the Vipps app, and many shops no longer handle much cash. Food shops close on Sundays, prices include 25 % VAT (moms), and receipts (kvittering) are needed for returns. Here are the words for the till, the price tag and your wallet.

    butikken, butikker, butikkene
    Butikken stenger klokka ni. The shop closes at nine.
