Sports & hobbies in Norwegian

Norway is a country of sport and outdoor life: cross-country skiing in winter, hiking in summer, football and handball for children all year. Joining a lag (club) or a kor (choir) is one of the best ways to make friends and practise the language. Note that Norwegians gå på ski, gå på tur and spille fotball.
