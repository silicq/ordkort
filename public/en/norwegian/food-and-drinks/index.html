Food & drinks in Norwegian

A Norwegian day of food often looks like this: brødskiver with cheese for breakfast, a matpakke at lunch, a warm middag around five and a light kveldsmat in the evening. Fish, potatoes and brown cheese are classics, and Friday is taco night in many homes. Here are the words for shopping and eating.

23 words

  • mat food
    maten, mater, matene
    Maten er klar! The food is ready!
  • et brød bread
    brødet, brød, brødene
    Jeg kjøper et grovbrød. I buy a loaf of wholemeal bread.
  • en ost cheese
    osten, oster, ostene
    Vil du ha ost på brødet? Would you like cheese on your bread?
  • smør butter
    smøret, smør, smørene
    Det er tomt for smør. We are out of butter.
  • et egg egg
    egget, egg, eggene
    Jeg spiser to egg til frokost. I eat two eggs for breakfast.
  • melk milk
    melken, melker, melkene
    Barna drikker melk. The children drink milk.
  • kaffe coffee
    kaffen, kaffer, kaffene
    Skal vi ta en kaffe? Shall we have a coffee?
  • te tea
    teen, teer, teene
    Jeg drikker te med honning. I drink tea with honey.
  • vann water
    vannet, vann, vannene
    Et glass vann, takk. A glass of water, please.
  • en juice juice
    juicen, juicer, juicene
    Barna drikker juice til frokost. The children drink juice at breakfast.
  • kjøtt meat
    kjøttet, kjøtt, kjøttene
    Spiser du kjøtt? Do you eat meat?
  • en kylling chicken
    kyllingen, kyllinger, kyllingene
    Vi har kylling til middag. We are having chicken for dinner.
  • en fisk fish
    fisken, fisker, fiskene
    Laks er en populær fisk. Salmon is a popular fish.
  • en potet potato
    poteta, poteter, potetene
    Vi spiser poteter nesten hver dag. We eat potatoes almost every day.
  • ris rice
    risen, riser, risene
    Kok ris i tjue minutter. Boil rice for twenty minutes.
  • en grønnsak vegetable
    grønnsaken, grønnsaker, grønnsakene
    Spis mer grønnsaker! Eat more vegetables!
  • frukt fruit
    frukta, frukter, fruktene
    Barna får frukt på skolen. The children get fruit at school.
  • et eple apple
    eplet, epler, eplene
    Vil du ha et eple? Would you like an apple?
  • ei suppe soup
    suppa, supper, suppene
    Suppa er for varm. The soup is too hot.
  • ei kake cake
    kaka, kaker, kakene
    Hun bakte ei kake. She baked a cake.
  • frokost breakfast
    frokosten, frokoster, frokostene
    Frokosten er klar. Breakfast is ready.
  • en matpakke packed lunch
    matpakken, matpakker, matpakkene
    Husk matpakka! Remember your packed lunch!
  • sulten hungry
    sultent, sultne, sultnere, sultnest
    Jeg er sulten! I’m hungry!
