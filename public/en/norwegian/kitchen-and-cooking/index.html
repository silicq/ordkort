Kitchen & cooking in Norwegian

Cooking at home is how most Norwegians eat — restaurants are expensive. Recipes use simple verbs: koke (boil), steke (fry or roast), skjære (cut), blande (mix). Measures are metric: a dl (decilitre) is 100 ml, a ss is a tablespoon and a ts a teaspoon. These words help you follow a recipe or help out in a kitchen job.
