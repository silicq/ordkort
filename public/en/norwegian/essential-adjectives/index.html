Essential adjectives in Norwegian

Norwegian adjectives agree with the noun in three ways: neuter adds -t (et stort hus), plural and definite add -e (store hus, det store huset). Many common adjectives are irregular in comparison — god, bedre, best; gammel, eldre, eldst — and a few never change at all, like bra and gratis. Here are the ones you will use every day.
