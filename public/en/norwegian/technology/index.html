Technology in Norwegian

Norway is one of the most digital countries in the world: you log in to the bank, NAV, the tax office and the doctor with BankID, pay with Vipps and get official letters in Digipost. Everyday tech vocabulary borrows from English — app, PC, wifi — but many words are Norwegian: mobil, lade, laste ned, passord.
