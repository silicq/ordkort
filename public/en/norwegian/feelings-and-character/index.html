Feelings & character in Norwegian

Norwegians often describe feelings with være and an adjective — Jeg er glad, Jeg er lei meg — or with reflexive verbs like glede seg (look forward), kjede seg (be bored) and føle seg (feel). Expressing feelings calmly is valued; a mild ikke så bra can mean quite a lot.
