Nature & weather in Norwegian

Nature is part of everyday life in Norway: fjords, mountains, forests and a coast that is longer than the equator if you count every island. The weather changes fast, so Norwegians talk about it constantly and check yr.no before every trip. Allemannsretten lets everyone walk and camp freely in uncultivated land.
