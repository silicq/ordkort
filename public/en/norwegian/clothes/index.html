Clothes in Norwegian

In Norway clothes are chosen for the weather more than for fashion: ull (wool) under everything in winter, a regnjakke for the rain, and joggesko or boots all year. Remember that bukse is singular — ei bukse is one pair of trousers — and that you take off your shoes at the door in Norwegian homes.
