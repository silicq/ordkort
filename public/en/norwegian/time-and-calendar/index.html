Time & calendar in Norwegian

Time words in Norwegian come with small but important prepositions: i dag, i går, i morgen, om sommeren, på mandag. Days and months are written with a small letter, and the clock is read with over and på: ti over fem, kvart på åtte. Remember that halv tre means 2:30 — half on the way to three.
