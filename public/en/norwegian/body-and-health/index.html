Body & health in Norwegian

At the doctor, at the pharmacy or when a child is ill, you need body words fast. Norwegians describe pain with vondt i: Jeg har vondt i hodet, vondt i magen. Note that Norwegian separates hånd (hand) from arm and fot (foot) from bein (leg), where Russian and Ukrainian use one word for each pair.
