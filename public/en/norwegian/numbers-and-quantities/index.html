Numbers & quantities in Norwegian

Numbers in Norwegian are regular once you know the pattern: tjueen, tjueto, tretti… Since 1951 the tens come first, though older people may still say “enogtjue”. Prices, phone numbers, ages and street addresses all use them, and Norwegians write a space between thousands and a comma for decimals: 12 500,50 kr.
