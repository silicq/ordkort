Family in Norwegian

Family words in Norwegian are short and logical: mor and far become mormor and farfar (mother’s mother, father’s father), and søsken covers brothers and sisters together. Many couples live together without marrying — the partner is called samboer. Possessives usually come after the noun: mannen min, barna våre.
