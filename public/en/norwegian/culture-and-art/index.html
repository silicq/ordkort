Culture & art in Norwegian

Norwegian culture mixes old traditions — bunad, folk music, Ibsen and Munch — with modern films, crime novels and music that travels the world. Libraries are free and lend books, films and even tools. Every municipality has a kulturskole for children, and many towns have a kulturhus with a cinema, a library and a concert hall.
