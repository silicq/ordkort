City & transport in Norwegian

Norwegian towns are compact and public transport is good: buses, trams, the T-bane in Oslo and boats along the coast. Tickets are bought in an app before you board. When asking the way, you will hear til høyre, til venstre and rett fram — and remember that cyclists and electric scooters share many streets.

    Vi bor i sentrum. We live in the centre.
