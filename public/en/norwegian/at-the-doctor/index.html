At the doctor in Norwegian

Everyone who lives in Norway has a fastlege (regular GP). You book a time on Helsenorge.no or by phone; outside office hours you call legevakten on 116 117, and in an emergency 113. At the pharmacy (apotek) you collect prescriptions with your ID. These words help you describe symptoms and understand the doctor.
