Colors & shapes in Norwegian

Colour words in Norwegian behave like other adjectives: en rød bil, et rødt hus, røde biler. A few, like blå and grå, are a little irregular, and some borrowed colours such as rosa and lilla never change. Shapes and sizes follow the same pattern — en stor, rund tallerken, et lite, firkantet bord.
