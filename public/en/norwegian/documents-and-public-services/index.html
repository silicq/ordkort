Documents & public services in Norwegian

Life in Norway runs through a handful of offices: UDI and the police for residence, Skatteetaten for your ID number and tax card, NAV for work and benefits, and the kommune for school, kindergarten and housing. Most things are done online with BankID. These words appear in every letter and form you will receive.
