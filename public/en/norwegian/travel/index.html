Travel in Norwegian

Travelling in Norway means long distances: planes between cities, the Hurtigruten ships along the coast, night trains and ferries across fjords. Many Norwegians spend holidays at a hytte (cabin) or abroad in the sun. These words cover booking, the airport, hotels and asking for help on the way.

22 words

  • ei reise trip, journey
    reisa, reiser, reisene
    God reise! Have a good trip!
  • å reise to travel
    reiser, reiste, har reist
    Vi reiser til Spania i juli. We are travelling to Spain in July.
  • en ferie holiday
    ferien, ferier, feriene
    Hvor skal du på ferie? Where are you going on holiday?
  • et fly plane
    flyet, fly, flyene
    Flyet går klokka sju. The plane leaves at seven.
  • en flyplass airport
    flyplassen, flyplasser, flyplassene
    Hvordan kommer jeg til flyplassen? How do I get to the airport?
  • en koffert suitcase
    kofferten, kofferter, koffertene
    Kofferten er for tung. The suitcase is too heavy.
  • et pass passport
    passet, pass, passene
    Husk passet! Remember your passport!
  • et hotell hotel
    hotellet, hotell, hotellene
    Vi bor på hotell i to netter. We are staying at a hotel for two nights.
  • ei hytte cabin
    hytten, hytter, hyttene
    Familien har ei hytte på fjellet. The family has a cabin in the mountains.
  • ei ferje ferry
    ferjen, ferjer, ferjene
    Ferja går hver halvtime. The ferry runs every half hour.
  • å bestille to book, to order
    bestiller, bestilte, har bestilt
    Jeg har bestilt rom på nettet. I have booked a room online.
  • å pakke to pack
    pakker, pakka, har pakka
    Har du pakket kofferten? Have you packed your suitcase?
  • en turist tourist
    turisten, turister, turistene
    Det er mange turister i Bergen om sommeren. There are many tourists in Bergen in summer.
  • utlandet abroad
    De reiser til utlandet hvert år. They travel abroad every year.
  • en grense border
    grensa, grenser, grensene

  • en avgang departure

    Neste avgang er klokka ti. The next departure is at ten.
  • forsinket delayed
    Flyet er forsinket en time. The plane is delayed by an hour.
  • en severdighet sight, attraction
    severdigheten, severdigheter, severdighetene
    Hvilke severdigheter bør vi se? Which sights should we see?
  • en strand beach
    stranda, strender, strendene
    Vi ligger på stranda hele dagen. We lie on the beach all day.
  • bagasje luggage
    bagasjen, bagasjer, bagasjene
    Hvor henter jeg bagasjen? Where do I collect my luggage?

  • å overnatte to stay the night
